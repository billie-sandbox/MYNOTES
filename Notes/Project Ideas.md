> [!tip] How to use
> - **New idea:** add a line at the top of *Inbox*: `- YYYY-MM-DD idea, plus anything you're thinking`. Add more lines under it later as sub-bullets.
> - **Ready to start or worth planning?** Make it a project: **Templater: Create Templates/New Dated Note** → *Project*, or a normal note from the Project Template. Then replace the line here with a link, e.g. `- [[My App]]`, and move it to *Promoted*.
> - **Dropped it?** Move it to *Parked* instead of deleting it.
